🗺️ World map

Hover (or tap) a vehicle or city. Dots = contract cities in the fleet's color (🟠 trucks, 🔵 tankers, 🟣 planes); a glowing ring = a vehicle is on its way there. Gray ring on a vehicle = waiting, red = broken down.

🚛 Semi-trucks

Buy a semi-truck (150,000) for one of your 15 slots. Each free truck gets 4 contract offers on roads around the world: pick one and it drives. When it arrives, the whole contract is paid to your money. Longer routes pay more per second. A truck earns back its price in about 1.5 hours. Trips keep moving while the game is closed.

🔧 Breakdowns: after each delivery there is a 1 in 100 chance the truck breaks down. It can't take new contracts until you pay for repairs (500 – 5,000).

🤖 Logistics agent (unlocks at Logistics level 5): turn it on and it picks a random contract for any waiting truck 5 seconds after the offers appear. It charges 2% of that contract's profit, taken from your money the moment it picks. It waits if you can't afford the fee.

🛢️ Crude oil tankers

Buy an Aframax tanker (75 scoin) for one of your 10 slots. Each free tanker gets 4 contract offers: pick one and the tanker sails. When it arrives, the whole contract is paid to your money. Then it gets 4 new offers. Longer routes pay more per second, and the two ocean crossings pay the most. A tanker earns back its price in about 3 hours. Voyages keep moving while the game is closed.

🤖 Logistics agent (unlocks at Logistics level 25): turn it on and it picks a random contract for any waiting tanker 5 seconds after the offers appear. It charges 2% of that contract's profit, taken from your money the moment it picks. It waits if you can't afford the fee.

✈️ Cargo planes

Buy a cargo plane (250 scoin) for one of your 5 slots. Unlocks when you own 3 oil tankers. Flights are short (2 – 8 minutes) and pay about 125 scoin per hour while flying, so a plane earns back its price in about 2 hours if you keep it busy. Each free plane gets 4 contract offers.

⚡ Express contracts: sometimes one offer is an express job: 40% faster and 25% more per second, but it disappears after 30 seconds if you don't take it.

🤖 Logistics agent (unlocks at Logistics level 50): picks a random contract 5 seconds after the offers appear, for 2% of that contract's profit.

📡 Live crypto market

● Connecting…
Trade Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana at their real, live prices (1 US dollar = 1 bcoin), streamed every second from Binance (or Coinbase if Binance isn't reachable). Prices move with the real market, 24/7, up or down.

Positions: every trade opens its own position. Long earns when the price goes up, Short when it goes down. Leverage ×20 / ×50 multiplies your profit and loss: at ×50, a 1% price move changes your position by 50%. If the price moves 1/leverage against you (5% at ×20, 2% at ×50; 100% up for a ×1 Short) the position is liquidated and you lose the money you put in. Liquidations are checked while the game is open.

Fee: 2% of the money you put in when you open and of what you get back when you close, 0.1% less for every 10 Investor levels (level 10, 20, 30…). At Investor level 99 trading is free.
XP: every trade gives Investor XP based on its size (1,000 → 1 XP, 1 scoin → 4 XP, 1 gcoin → 10 XP), at most once a minute. Coins you hold also count as invested money for the per-minute Investor XP. Min. trade 1,000. Trading pauses if the live prices stop.
