The controller of your personal data for the game Empire of Coins (the "Game") is Aleksandrs Ozolins, contact@empireofcoins.com. This policy explains what data the Game uses, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

As a guest, your game progress and settings are stored only in your own browser (local storage). We do not receive them. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Technical data when you open the Game

Like any website, the Game cannot load without your device connecting to the servers that deliver it. These providers receive your IP address and basic browser information and may keep it briefly in their logs for security and operation (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosts the Game and delivers it through its global network.
  • Supabase Inc.: account and cloud save database (only if you sign in), hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google: Google sign-in (only if you use it) and the Google Fonts typeface used in the Game.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the sign-in software library.
  • Ko-fi (and PayPal or Stripe): only if you choose to donate. You leave the Game and pay on Ko-fi's website; Ko-fi shares your name or display name, your email address, the amount and any message you write with us so we can see and thank you for the donation. We never receive your card or bank details. Legal basis: performance of the donation you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); donation records are kept for as long as tax law requires.
  • Binance and Coinbase: public cryptocurrency price feeds shown in the 📈 Investing section (prices only; no account is used).

Some of these providers are based in, or may transfer data to, countries outside the European Economic Area such as the United States. Such transfers rely on safeguards recognised by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Game uses no advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across websites. It only uses your browser's local storage for the purposes described above, which are strictly necessary to run the Game you requested.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud save: until you delete your account.
  • Provider server logs: for the short period set by each provider for security purposes.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and cloud save at any time in ⚙️ Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and saved progress from our database. You can also ask us by email.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact contact@empireofcoins.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live (in Spain: the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

9. Age

The Game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data of minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced in the Game.
